Air Pilot Planner

Plan a roof scan here, then send it to the phone.

1 · Trace the roof edge

Click the two ends of the roof's lower edge (the gutter line) on the face you want to scan. The drone flies on the side with the yellow arrow.

1 · Outline the house

Click each corner of the house (or roof). Click the first corner again, or press Done.

2 · Roof and flight

Heights are from where the drone takes off. Take off from the ground next to the house. A single-story roof is about 10 ft at the gutter and 16–20 ft at the peak.

3 · Check the plan

Draw on the map to see the flight.

4 · Send to the phone

For Google Drive: download the file, save it in Drive, then on the phone open Missions → Import and pick it.
